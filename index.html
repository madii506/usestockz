02 · How it pays

01 / 05

Launch a coin for any wallet.

Paste the wallet that gets paid, pick its stock, name the coin. A fresh coin wallet creates it on pump.fun, so your own wallet never shows up as the creator.

02 / 05

Every trade pays a fee.

Every buy and sell on the coin pays pump.fun's creator fee. On Stockz, that fee belongs to the account you launched it for.

03 / 05

Fees buy the stock.

Every hour, the coin's fees are swapped into its stock at the live price, routed by Jupiter. SOL in, NVDA out.

04 / 05

Sealed on the way.

The stock moves through Umbra, on Arcium. The sender, the receiver and the amount are encrypted on Solana. Anyone can see that something moved. Nobody can see what, or how much.

11 · Questions

The short answers.

What is a stock account?

Any Solana wallet you launch a coin for. Its coin's creator fees buy one stock and pay it into that wallet's private balance.

Who can I launch a coin for?

Any Solana wallet: yours, a friend's, a creator's, a charity's. The wallet you paste is the one that gets paid, and it can't be changed after launch.

Which stocks can it pay in?

NVIDIA, Apple, Tesla, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Coinbase, the S&P 500, the Nasdaq 100, gold and silver, as tokenized xStocks on Solana. You pick one per coin.

What exactly is private?

The payout moves through Umbra, which runs on Arcium. On Solana, the sender, the receiver and the amount are encrypted. The coin itself and its trades are public on pump.fun, like every coin.

How does the account get the stock?

It waits in that wallet's private balance on Umbra. Only that wallet can see it, keep it there or move it out.

How often does it pay?

Every hour, the coin's creator fees are swapped into its stock and sent. Small amounts wait for the next hour so network fees don't eat them.

Is it live?

Live today: stock prices, the calculator, the wallet X-ray and launch drafts. Next: launches, then payouts. Nothing on this site shows a coin until launches open.

12 · Risks

Read this before you launch.

Payouts follow trading.

No trades, no fees, no stock. Most coins stop trading.

Stocks move.

The stock can fall after it's bought. Tokenized stocks can trade off the market price when liquidity is thin.
