01 · Overview

Stockz docs

Stockz is a private launchpad that pays any stock account. You launch a coin for any Solana wallet and pick a stock. Every hour, the coin's creator fees buy that stock and pay it into the wallet's private balance, encrypted on Solana.

Three ideas carry it: anyone can be paid (any wallet, chosen at launch), fees become real stocks (tokenized, backed one to one), and nobody else sees the payout (sender, receiver and amount sealed).

02 · How a payout works

How a payout works

StepWhat happens
1. LaunchA fresh coin wallet creates the coin on pump.fun with your name, ticker and image. The stock account and the stock are fixed for good.
2. TradesEvery buy and sell pays pump.fun's creator fee to the coin's creator: the coin wallet.
3. SwapEvery hour the engine claims those fees and swaps them into the coin's stock through Jupiter, at the live price.
4. SealThe stock is sent through Umbra, which runs on Arcium. On Solana, the sender, the receiver and the amount are encrypted.
5. HoldThe stock waits in the stock account's private balance. Only that wallet can see it, keep it or move it out.

03 · Stock accounts

Stock accounts

A stock account is any Solana wallet you launch a coin for: your own, a friend's, a creator's or a charity's. One coin pays one account in one stock. Both are written into the launch and can't be changed afterwards, by you or by us.

i

The account opens its private balance in Umbra with the same wallet. Nothing is sent to an exchange or a custodian on the way.

05 · Privacy

What stays private

Sealed on SolanaStill public
Who sent a payoutThat a payment happened
Which wallet received itThe coin and every trade on pump.fun
How much stock movedThe coin wallet that created the coin
The account's private balanceFees swapped into the stock before they're sealed

Payouts run through Umbra, a privacy layer on Solana that has been open to everyone since March 2026. Umbra runs on Arcium, an encrypted computer on Solana mainnet. Stockz is independent of both.

06 · The coin wallet

The coin wallet

Every coin gets its own fresh wallet. That wallet creates the coin, receives its creator fees and runs its payouts, so your wallet never appears as the coin's creator or dev. You fund the launch from your wallet in one payment.

07 · Fees and timing

Fees and timing

pump.fun sets the creator fee, and it changes with a coin's market cap. The swap into the stock pays Jupiter's route costs and Solana network fees. Payouts go out every hour; amounts too small to be worth a transaction wait for the next hour.

08 · Live tools

Live tools

Stock prices come from Jupiter every minute. Wallet X-ray reads any wallet's public stock holdings straight from Solana, the view Stockz payouts keep empty. The calculator turns a coin's daily volume into shares at live prices. Launch drafts build the coin's card as you type and save it in your browser.

11 · Risks

Risks

  • Payouts follow trading. No trades, no fees, no stock.
  • Stocks move. A stock can fall after it's bought, and tokenized stocks can trade away from the market price when liquidity is thin.
  • Software can fail. The engine, pump.fun, Jupiter, Umbra and Arcium are all software.
  • Not affiliated. Stockz is independent of Umbra, Arcium, xStocks, Jupiter and pump.fun.
